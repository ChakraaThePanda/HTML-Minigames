Key Tiles

Tiles stream down every lane, but only one is active at a time — the one outlined in white. Press its lane's key (or tap that lane on mobile) anywhere along the lane, no need to wait for the bottom. Clearing tiles out of order, pressing the wrong key, or letting the active tile reach the bottom all end the run. Speed climbs continuously — your score is how many tiles per second you were clearing when it ended.
